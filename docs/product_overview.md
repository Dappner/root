# Root: Product Overview

## What Root Is

Root is a personal knowledge system for capturing, organizing, and revisiting information from books, articles, conversations, and voice memos. It acts as a durable, long-term memory substrate.

Root is **AI-assisted**, not AI-driven. You make the decisions; AI handles structure, summarization, suggestions, and recall support.

---

## Core Concepts

* **Sources** – Books, articles, podcasts, memos.
* **Sections** – User-defined subdivisions per source.
* **Citations** – Quotes, passages, excerpts.
* **Captures** – Your thoughts tied to citations.
* **Voice Memos** – Audio input → transcript → suggested captures/citations.

---

## System Principles

* Low-friction capture
* Context preservation
* Structured recall
* Compounding insights
* Clean data model: every capture links to a citation, citations belong to sections, sections belong to sources

---

## Activation Model

* Visiting a source or adding a capture updates `last_active_at`.
* "Mark as done" sets `archived_at`.
* Queries differentiate active, recently active, archived.

---

## What Root Enables

* Reliable long-term memory of what you read and think.
* A place where ideas accumulate rather than disappear.
* An extensible foundation for future knowledge tools.
