# Root: Philosophy & Long-Term Goals

## Philosophy

Root is built on a simple belief: **technology should enhance your ability to think, not replace it**.

### Guiding Principles

* **Human-first** – Your judgment, your understanding. AI supports, clarifies, and structures.
* **Low friction** – Knowledge should enter the system easily.
* **High fidelity** – Original source context is preserved.
* **Incremental refinement** – Insights evolve through repeated recall and synthesis.
* **Compounding value** – The more you add, the more valuable the system becomes.

---

## Long-Term Vision

A personal knowledge environment that:

* remembers what you’ve read
* understands how your ideas connect
* helps you resurface and strengthen insights over time
* provides structure without imposing it

---

## Feature Roadmap

### Near-Term

* Streamlined capture → citation flow
* Section-based source organization
* Recall prompts for recently active sources
* Cleaned activation model

### Mid-Term

* Audio pipeline: voice memo → transcript → capture/quote suggestions
* Robust section grouping from backend
* Enrichment pipeline: summaries, context extraction
* Section-focused subviews to zoom into specific parts of a source

### Long-Term

* Knowledge graph linking ideas across sources
* Semantic recall and resurfacing
* Multi-modal ingestion (PDF, images, audio)
* Personalized insights: recurring themes, long-term interests
* Optional collaborative annotation

---

## Why This Matters

Root aims to become a **trusted cognitive partner**—a system that captures your intellectual history, supports your ongoing thinking, and helps knowledge accumulate rather than evaporate.

AI becomes an assistant to your curiosity, not a replacement for your mind.
